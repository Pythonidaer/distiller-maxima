Why this exists

Johnny saved more than 200 freeCodeCamp bookmarks across programming, careers, data, and AI. Each one looked worth reading, but the collection as a whole was difficult to process. Where did the topics overlap? What belonged together? What might be a useful place to begin?

Distiller Maxima grew out of that question. It organizes the collective information into roadmaps and reading guides, so it is easier to get a coherent view before spending more time with a particular subject. The original articles remain the place to go for the full explanation.

From bookmarks to guides

Johnny provided the page titles to ChatGPT, then worked through how the resources should be grouped. Those titles were matched to full source links. Written resource pages were reviewed to create original commentary that connects related ideas and explains what a reader might gain from each topic.

The guides aim for a friendly, plain-language voice: enough context to help you choose a direction, without making every topic feel like another prerequisite. Headings, small practice suggestions, and links to the original sources let you decide how deeply to read.

This is a reading companion, not a replacement for the sources. Video courses were not watched, and tutorial code was not run. The methodology and writing and navigation rules explain the approach and its limits.

Roadmap design

The roadmap designs borrow the visual approach of roadmap.sh for educational purposes only: a central path, topic branches, and linked reading. This is an independent learning project, with no affiliation or endorsement implied.

The sequence is a suggestion, not a curriculum you must finish in order. Choose a branch that interests you, use the guide to orient yourself, and return to the original resources when you want more depth.

The article collection

A structured article collection connects each resource’s title and source URL to its roadmap, group, and topic. Stable IDs help connect that information across the diagrams and reading guides.

This JSON data is part of the collection and synthesis workflow. It provides the source mapping behind the site; the original commentary lives in the guides. Links to the collection and the Markdown rules are available in every footer.
