Fonts

The interface uses Arial, with Helvetica and the browser’s sans-serif as fallbacks. Grime Slime Dripping appears only in the landing hero. Methodology text and hero CTA labels use the system monospace stack.

The display font is self-hosted as GrimeSlimeDripping-Regular.ttf with font-display: swap. The downloaded demo is marked for personal use. Its supplied notice is included for reference.

Typography roles

These roles are shared across the app through design-system.css. Font families stay the same, while sizes, line heights, weights, spacing, and color responsibilities follow a common baseline. A line height without units is a multiplier of the font size. “Normal” letter spacing uses the font’s default spacing.

Fluid sizes use clamp(minimum, viewport-based size, maximum). At a 1440px viewport, the hero title is 86.4px with a 108px line height. The title stays on one line from 1000px wide and can wrap below that.

Article body copy uses 16px and a 1.75 line height at widths up to 600px and between 640–900px. Elsewhere it uses 18px with the same line-height multiplier. Active outline links and main roadmap nodes gain weight to show hierarchy.

Spacing and header behavior

The spacing scale is 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, and 64px. Paragraphs use 24px below them, subsections use 48px, and major article sections use 64px. Roadmap canvas coordinates stay fixed so connectors continue to align with their nodes.

On the landing page, navigation is translucent with light text and a 5px backdrop blur with a feathered lower edge over the dark hero. It switches to the white reading header when the 60% point of the hero reaches the bottom of the sticky header. Dropdowns and the mobile navigation panel remain solid white. Other pages use the white header from the start.

The same semantic color tokens cover paper, hero, ink, muted text, dividers, accent, roadmap path, roadmap hover, and selected surfaces. Change a token to update that role across the interface.

Palette

The palette combines white reading pages, near-black hero space, gray text and surfaces, neon pink accents, and blue roadmap paths. Neutral shades serve named surface, border, and text roles. Blue is reserved for roadmap relationships and node feedback, while pink marks links and actions.

Transparent layers

The sticky header uses white at 90% opacity, increasing to 96% after scrolling. The menu shadow uses #20212412, approximately 7% opacity. The dialog backdrop uses #17171099, or 60% opacity.

Pink on white is used for decorative underlines rather than small body text. Reading text remains ink or muted gray. Roadmap blue identifies paths, while node labels stay dark.
